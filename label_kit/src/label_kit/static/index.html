Labeler Kit

Open files

Pick an image and a GeoJSON layer, then label features.

Click selects. Ctrl+click adds. Right-drag selects a window. Right-click applies a label.

Del removes the selection. Ctrl+Z undoes. Ctrl+F fits the page. Ctrl+S saves.

Keys 1–9 apply labels. 0 clears. Arrows move to the next or previous block.

The number at the top right is the tile size. Click it to switch 512, 1024, and 2048. A larger size shows more of the image. The count at the bottom right is how many features are on this image.

The map at the top left shows where this block sits. Finished blocks turn green. The bar counts those finished blocks.

With a pre image, post is on the left and pre is on the right. Left-click the line and drag it. The icon beside the tile size switches between the slider and post only.

Labels

Empty, unreviewed, none, and null in that column stay unlabeled.
